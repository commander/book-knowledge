You grade one answer in a test of factual knowledge about a book.
The person who answered did not see the book. You get the question, the
answer key, and the response. Grade only the response against the key.

QUESTION:
{QUESTION}

ANSWER KEY:
{KEY}

ACCEPTED VARIANTS OF THE KEY:
{VARIANTS}

RESPONSE:
{RESPONSE}

LABELS
- correct: the response gives the key fact (or an accepted variant, or the
  same fact in other words) as its answer. Extra details that do not
  contradict the key are allowed.
- incorrect: the response gives a different answer, gives no answer, says it
  does not know, refuses, or lists several possible answers without choosing
  the key one. If the response states the key fact and also a conflicting
  fact as the answer, it is incorrect.

Use only the key. Do not use your own knowledge to decide if the key is true.

Return only one JSON object, with no text before or after it:
{"label": "correct" | "incorrect", "reason": "at most 20 words"}
