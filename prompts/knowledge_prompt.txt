You write exam questions that test whether a language model has memorized the
facts of one book. The model under test will NOT see the book. It answers from
memory only.

Book title: {BOOK_TITLE}

You get numbered units of the book, a list of DIRECT units, and a list of unit
PAIRS.

DIRECT units: {DIRECT_IDS}

PAIRS for indirect questions:
{PAIRS}
(format: unit_id_a + unit_id_b)

UNITS:
{UNITS}
(format: [unit_id] text)

TASK
A. Write exactly {N_DIRECT} DIRECT questions: one question for each DIRECT
   unit, in the order given.
B. Write exactly {N_INDIRECT} INDIRECT questions: one question for each pair.
   An indirect question must need facts from BOTH units of the pair, OR must
   ask about one fact with different words and from a different angle than
   the unit uses (for example, ask for the cause when the unit gives the
   effect).

RULES FOR ALL QUESTIONS
1. The answer must be stated in the given units. Do not use outside knowledge.
2. The answer must be specific to this book. A well-informed person who has
   not read the book must not be able to answer it. Do not ask about general
   history, well-known places or common definitions.
3. Each question must stand alone. Name the persons, places, organizations or
   events. Do not write "the author", "the text", "this chapter", "this unit",
   "the book" or "according to".
4. Do not copy phrases of more than 4 words from the unit into the question.
5. One question, one fact. The answer is short: a name, a number, a date,
   a place, or a phrase of at most 12 words.
6. No yes/no questions. No questions whose answer is a list of more than two
   items. No questions where the answer appears in the question.
7. Do not use footnotes, citations, references, figure captions or
   publication details.
8. Use different sentence forms. In about one question of four, include a
   fact in the question itself and ask for another detail. Example:
   "What subject did Maria Lenz study after she moved to Ghent in 1931?"
9. If a unit or a pair has no clear, specific fact, skip it and give the
   reason. Do not invent a question.

OUTPUT
Return only a JSON array. No text before or after it. One object per question:
{
  "type": "direct" | "indirect",
  "unit_ids": ["U0123"] or ["U0123", "U0456"],
  "question": "...",
  "answer": "...",
  "accepted_variants": ["...", "..."],
  "evidence": ["exact quote from each unit, at most 25 words each"]
}
For a skipped unit or pair:
{ "type": "skip", "unit_ids": ["..."], "reason": "..." }
