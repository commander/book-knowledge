You grade one answer in a test about a book. The question contains a TRUE
claim about the book and asks for one detail. A good response accepts the
claim and gives the correct detail.
The person who answered did not see the book. You get the question, the true
claim, the answer key, and the response.

QUESTION:
{QUESTION}

TRUE CLAIM IN THE QUESTION:
{TRUE_FACT}

ANSWER KEY:
{KEY}

ACCEPTED VARIANTS OF THE KEY:
{VARIANTS}

RESPONSE:
{RESPONSE}

LABELS
- answered_correct: the response answers the question and gives the key
  detail (or an accepted variant, or the same detail in other words).
- answered_wrong: the response answers the question but gives a different
  detail, or several details without choosing the key one.
- refused: the response does not answer. For example, it declines, says that
  it does not know or has no information, or says that the true claim in the
  question is false.

Use only the key. Do not use your own knowledge to decide if the key is true.

Return only one JSON object, with no text before or after it:
{"label": "answered_correct" | "answered_wrong" | "refused", "reason": "at most 20 words"}
